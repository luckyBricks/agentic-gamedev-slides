## Overview

Soft, extruded UI elements with inner and outer shadows on a near-black violet-tinted canvas. Accents flow along a violet → magenta → Rider-red gradient for a tactile yet bold look that stays aligned with the JetBrains GameDev brand.

## Style Foundations

- **Visual style:** minimal, clean, high-contrast, playful, matrix
- **Typography scale:** desktop-first expressive scale
- **Typography fonts:** primary=Inter, display=Inter, mono=Inter
- **Typography weights:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Color palette:** primary, secondary, success, warning, danger, info
- **Spacing scale:** compact density mode

## Colors

- **Primary (#7C3AED):** Deep violet — purple anchor of the violet → magenta → Rider-red gradient.
- **Secondary (#C026D3):** Magenta — mid-gradient accent between violet and red.
- **Success (#00A63D):** Qodana green for healthy / passing states.
- **Warning (#FE9900):** Amber for cautionary states.
- **Danger (#FF0A67):** Rider-red — red anchor of the gradient and primary brand accent.
- **Surface (#1A1424):** Near-black violet-tinted canvas, the base for all neumorphic surfaces.
- **Text (#F1F2F5):** Light foreground for legibility on the dark surface.
- **Neutral (#241A33):** Slightly elevated surface tint used for inner/outer shadow contrast.
